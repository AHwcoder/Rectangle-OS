# Rectangle OS

"Freedom in every rectangle."

## Vision
Rectangle OS is an ambitious operating system born from the heart of Jordan, striving to be the first system with an integrated native origin. Our goal is to provide a technical experience that imposes no restrictions on the user, granting complete sovereignty over their interface, resources, and digital identity.

## Why Rectangle OS?
Users have grown tired of traditional systems that consume resources pointlessly and impose rigid, unchangeable designs. We are here to change the rules:
* **Absolute Control:** A dynamic user interface that gives you complete freedom to customize the taskbar and elements, placing them wherever you wish (top, bottom, right, left).
* **Performance Lightness:** Designed from scratch to minimize RAM consumption to the maximum extent, ensuring a smooth experience even on mid-range devices.
* **An Identity That Understands You:** An operating system designed to support the Arabic language and its culture deep within the system, rather than just being a translated interface.
* **Solid Security:** In Rectangle OS, we rely on a high-level abstraction system to ensure privacy and security, reducing the need for cumbersome traditional antiviruses.
* **Extensibility:** The core version is available for developers and systems experts to modify, customize, and build their own versions. We do not sell a system; we are establishing a technical community.

## Roadmap
We are in the foundational phase, working hard to build the kernel that will carry all these ambitions.
* **Phase One:** Building the core kernel using Rust to ensure safety and speed.
* **Phase Two:** Developing the interactive system interface (The Rectangle UI).
* **Phase Three:** Integrating the resource management system to achieve maximum efficiency in memory consumption. 
*(We are currently in Phase One)*

## Share the Dream
Rectangle OS started from the vision of a single person in Jordan, but it aspires to be a platform that brings together highly experienced minds. If you are a systems developer or interested in building the future of software, we invite you to contribute with us.
